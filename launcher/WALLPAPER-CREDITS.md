# Wallpaper credits

Photos from Wikimedia Commons Featured Pictures, used under their free licences. Each photo keeps its
own licence; the MIT licence of the code does not cover them.

**Changes:** every image was resized to 1920×1080 for the launcher. Where the original is not 16:9
(noted below, from the original's size on Commons), it was also cropped to 16:9. The Rio de Janeiro
original is in the ProPhoto RGB colour space and was converted to sRGB so it shows correctly on a TV.
Nothing else was changed. The resized and cropped copies are shared under the same licence as their
original.
